Privacy notice

Website matter-fit submissions

This notice explains how PULSE Advisory Group handles information submitted through its public website form.

Information collected

The form requests your name, work email, role, firm or organization, jurisdiction, decision timeline, and a non-identifying description of the matter type and decision context. The form is not intended to collect patient information, medical records, personnel records, or other protected or confidential information.

Purpose

PULSE uses submitted information to assess potential fit, identify conflicts or scope questions, reply to the inquiry, and maintain reasonable business and administrative records. Submission does not create an attorney-client, expert, advisory, or contractual relationship.

Form processor and international processing

The form is processed by Formspree, Inc. Formspree states that it may collect and store submitted personal information and technical information such as IP address, browser type, access time, and referring website. Formspree states that information may be processed in the United States and other countries where it operates. Review the Formspree Privacy Policy before using the form.

Retention and access

PULSE keeps inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to evaluate fit, communicate with you, maintain conflict and administrative records, and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by PULSE, email jen@prismqd.ai with the subject “PULSE privacy request.” Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, conflict, security, or recordkeeping obligations.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, dates of service, record excerpts, personnel files, or other protected or confidential information. The public form and ordinary email are not secure channels for case records. If there is a potential fit, secure-transfer instructions will follow after conflicts and scope are reviewed.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to jen@prismqd.ai.

Effective date: August 23, 2026
