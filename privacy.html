Privacy notice

Website and report-tool information handling

This notice explains how PULSE Advisory Group handles information submitted through its public matter-fit form and the Workforce Exposure Report test flow.

Matter-fit information collected

The public matter-fit form requests your name, work email, role, firm or organization, jurisdiction, decision timeline, and a non-identifying description of the matter type and decision context. The form is not intended to collect patient information, medical records, personnel records, or other protected or confidential information.

Matter-fit purpose

PULSE uses submitted matter-fit information to assess potential fit, identify conflicts or scope questions, reply to the inquiry, and maintain reasonable business and administrative records. Submission does not create an attorney-client, expert, advisory, or contractual relationship.

Matter-fit form processor and international processing

The matter-fit form is processed by Formspree, Inc. Formspree states that it may collect and store submitted personal information and technical information such as IP address, browser type, access time, and referring website. Formspree states that information may be processed in the United States and other countries where it operates. Review the Formspree Privacy Policy before using the form.

Workforce Exposure Report test flow

The Workforce Exposure Report requests a non-identifying institution or facility label, intended recipient label, decision context, scope and denominator selections, numeric scenario inputs, a decision question, source/provenance notes, and optional non-identifying assumptions. These inputs are for analytical scenario screening only. Do not enter patient data, medical records, personnel records, credentials, trade secrets, privileged material, or other protected or confidential information.

When you preview the report, the current implementation stores the report-form values in the browser tab's sessionStorage under the PULSE site origin so the same tab can restore the values after a test-checkout redirect. Session storage is browser-local and is normally removed when that tab or browser session ends, but browser behavior can vary. Anyone with access to the same active browser session may be able to view the restored form values. Do not use the report tool for sensitive information or on a shared or untrusted browser session.

The report tool does not intentionally collect or store payment-card details in PULSE browser storage. Checkout creation sends no report-form contents in the checkout request body. The test checkout redirects you to Stripe, which handles checkout and payment information under Stripe's own privacy terms. PULSE receives or verifies transaction/session and entitlement state needed to determine whether the paid test report may be generated.

After a redeemed test entitlement is verified, the report-form values are sent to the same-origin PULSE report API to generate the analytical HTML response. The application is designed to keep entitlement and replay-control state separate from report contents. Do not treat the current test flow as a secure records-intake channel.

Retention and access

PULSE keeps matter-fit inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to evaluate fit, communicate with you, maintain conflict and administrative records, and meet applicable obligations. Test-checkout and entitlement records may be retained as reasonably needed for payment verification, replay prevention, security, troubleshooting, and administrative records. Browser session storage is controlled by the browser session rather than by PULSE's server-side retention process. To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by PULSE, email jen@prismqd.ai with the subject “PULSE privacy request.” Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, conflict, security, payment, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping obligations.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical-record numbers, dates of service, record excerpts, personnel files, credentials, privileged material, or other protected or confidential information in the public form or Workforce Exposure Report. The website, report test flow, and ordinary email are not secure channels for case records. If there is a potential fit, secure-transfer instructions will follow after conflicts and scope are reviewed.
