See the event in the context of the system around it.

PULSE helps counsel organize clinical events, escalation pathways, staffing conditions, communication, governance, and available records into a clearer operating picture.

The work is independent, evidence-calibrated, and designed to identify questions that routine chart abstraction may not reach. PULSE does not determine negligence, causation, or legal liability.

Request a Matter-Fit Review

Fit-based intake followed, when appropriate, by a scoped proposal. Do not send records or protected health information through the website form.

Who it is for

For counsel deciding what the record does—and does not—explain.

A medical record can document actions without fully explaining how recognition, staffing, communication, escalation, authority, and follow-up shaped the event. PULSE examines those layers together and distinguishes documented fact, interpretation, unresolved question, and information still needed.

Process

Scope before records.

1. Matter-fit review

Submit role, organization, timing, and a non-identifying description of the decision context.

2. Conflict and scope review

PULSE determines fit and identifies the information required for a proposal.

3. Written proposal

If there is fit, the proposal defines deliverables, exclusions, fees, timing, confidentiality, information transfer, and intended use.

4. Secure intake and analysis

Records are accepted only through the agreed secure process after engagement terms are in place. Analysis distinguishes fact, interpretation, hypothesis, limitation, and open question.
